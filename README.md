# Field Visit Management Module & Mobile Application

## Project Overview

The **Field Visit Management System** is a centralized solution designed to manage, monitor, and streamline field activities, customer visits, and on-site business interactions.

The solution consists of a web-based Field Visit Management Module and a dedicated Mobile Application, enabling employees and managers to coordinate field visits, record customer interactions, track visit outcomes, and maintain structured field activity records.

The platform focuses on improving field team accountability, visit planning, operational visibility, and communication between field employees and management.

The mobile application extends the functionality of the web platform by allowing employees to manage and update field activities while working in the field.

## Core Functionalities

- **Field Visit Planning:** Creation and scheduling of field visits, including visit details, planned dates, and assigned employees.
- **Visit Assignment:** Assignment and coordination of field visits among employees and teams.
- **Customer & Location Management:** Management of customer details and visit-related location information.
- **Visit Tracking:** Monitoring of planned, ongoing, and completed field visits.
- **Meeting Management:** Recording customer meetings, discussion points, and interaction details.
- **Minutes of Meeting (MOM):** Documentation of meeting discussions, decisions, and important observations.
- **Visit Outcomes:** Recording visit results, customer responses, and follow-up requirements.
- **Expense Management:** Recording and tracking expenses associated with field visits.
- **Visit History:** Centralized records of previous visits and employee field activities.
- **Reporting & Analytics:** Monitoring visit completion, employee activities, visit outcomes, and field operations.

## Mobile Application

A dedicated mobile application has been developed to extend field visit functionality beyond the desktop environment.

**Key Capabilities:**
- Mobile access to assigned field visits.
- Field visit information and schedule viewing.
- On-the-go visit status updates.
- Customer meeting and interaction records.
- MOM and visit outcome submissions.
- Field expense recording.
- Access to relevant field activity information.

The mobile application enables employees to manage their field operations conveniently without depending on the web application for every activity.

## User Roles

| Role | Description |
|---|---|
| Admin | Manages system configuration, users, and overall field visit operations. |
| Manager / Team Lead | Plans and assigns visits, monitors team activities, and reviews visit outcomes. |
| Employee / Field Executive | Views assigned visits, manages field activities, records customer interactions, and submits visit reports. |
