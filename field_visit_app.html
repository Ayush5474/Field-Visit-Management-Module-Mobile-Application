Field Visit App

Prototype for the developer team. Employees do the field work. Team Leads and Managers see their whole team and approve from the same app.

Employee: a visit assigned in the CRM appears here. Select it, Check in, work, Check out. Expenses, notes and details are optional.
Team Lead and Manager: see every employee, where they are today and what is waiting for approval. Approve reports, expense claims and reschedule requests.
Approval chain: Team Lead, then Manager. A Manager approval completes all remaining levels.
Try it: sign in as an employee, check in, then sign out and sign in as a Team Lead or Manager. Keep the CRM module open in another tab: both stay in sync.

No real login, GPS or map. Locations are simulated.
